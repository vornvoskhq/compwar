High-Capacity Dual-Section Air Variable Tuning Capacitor with Shaft Collar

Product Overview

The 922111-2 is a high-capacitance dual-gang air variable capacitor delivering approximately 575 pF per section. It is specifically
designed for high-inductance tuning networks, low-frequency RF circuits, antenna matching units (transmatches), and AM/broadcast
receiving gear requiring large capacitance swings.
