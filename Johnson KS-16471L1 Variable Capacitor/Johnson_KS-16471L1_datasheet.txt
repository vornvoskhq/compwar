E.F. Johnson KS-16471L1 Air Variable Capacitor
E.F. Johnson Company / Western Electric Kearny Specification (KS-16471 L1)

Product Overview

The E.F. Johnson KS-16471L1 is a telecommunications and military-grade air variable capacitor manufactured for Western Electric
under Kearny Specification KS-16471. Utilizing Johnson Type L construction, it features dual steatite ceramic end plates joined by
rigid metal support rods, providing exceptional mechanical rigidity and thermal stability.
