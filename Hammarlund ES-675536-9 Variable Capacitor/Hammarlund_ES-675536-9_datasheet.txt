Product Overview

The Hammarlund ES-675536-9 is a precision air dielectric trimmer capacitor manufactured by Hammarlund in New York. Based on
the venerable APC/HF series architecture, it utilizes a silicone-treated steatite ceramic base plate, nickel-plated brass stator and rotor
plates, and a threaded panel mounting bushing with an extended rotor control shaft.
