Knight 1255 / RVC273 Dual-Gang Variable Capacitor
Allied Radio Knight-Kit / Radio Condenser Co. (EIA Source Code 282)

Product Overview

The Knight 1255 (manufactured by Radio Condenser Company under EIA code 282) is a precision dual-gang air variable capacitor
designed for broadcast AM, shortwave receivers, and communications equipment. It features an integrated large-diameter stamped
metal drive wheel intended for dial cord vernier drive systems, providing smooth, high-precision frequency tuning across its 13 pF to
405 pF capacitance span.
