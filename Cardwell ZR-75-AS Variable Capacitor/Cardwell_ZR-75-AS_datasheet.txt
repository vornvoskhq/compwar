Cardwell ZR-75-AS Trim-Air Variable Capacitor
The Allen D. Cardwell Manufacturing Corporation / Cardwell Condenser Corp

Product Overview

The Cardwell ZR-75-AS is a precision Trim-Air series variable capacitor engineered for high-frequency VHF/UHF oscillator, receiver,
and transmitter trimmer applications. Built with a high-grade steatite end plate, it minimizes dielectric absorption and dielectric
heating while maintaining mechanical stability under vibration.
