Product Overview

The E.F. Johnson 153-210 is a heavy-duty, commercial-grade Type D (153 series) 3-gang air variable capacitor. Designed for
multi-stage RF amplifiers, multi-band receiver front-ends, and transmitter stages, it features 0.051 inch thick aluminum plates with
rounded edges to prevent corona discharge, supported on massive 5/16 inch aluminum tie rods and steatite insulators.
